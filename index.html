Our Story

A Royal House of Thai Cooking

🪷

Authentic & Traditional

“Ruen Bongkot” means House of the Lotus. We lean into authentic, traditional royal flavors — classic Thai comfort food alongside regional specialties you won't find at a standard takeout joint.

🌶️

Fresh, With Real Heat

Our kitchen is praised for fresh ingredients and great heat control — every dish made to order, from mild and fragrant to Thai-hot for the brave.

🏡

A Westchester Favorite

On the bustling Central Park Avenue strip between Scarsdale and Edgemont, we're an easy stop for neighbors from Hartsdale, Yonkers, Eastchester and beyond — with a stellar 4.7-star local rating.
